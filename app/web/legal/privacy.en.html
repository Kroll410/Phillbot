1. Who we are and what this document covers

PhillBot ({{PUBLIC_URL}}) is a free tool for Twitch streamers, available by invitation only (allowlist). It lets a streamer:

  • collect game orders paid with channel points ("SubDay");
  • run an auction with a wheel ("Viewing Day");
  • run a "1/2" chat poll;
  • show a timer widget in OBS;
  • accept Donatello donations as orders or bids.

The service is run by {{OPERATOR}}, an individual in Ukraine ("we"). This page explains what data we collect, why, where and for how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted. Questions: {{CONTACT}}.

2. What data we process

2.1. Streamers (people who sign in)

  • Twitch id, login and display name;
  • Twitch OAuth tokens — stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM);
  • session cookie — only its hash is stored in the database;
  • time of last sign-in;
  • settings, including the Donatello callback key and reward IDs;
  • access log: sign-in, refusal, adding to and removing from the list — with timestamps.

We request these permissions from Twitch: channel:read:redemptions, channel:read:subscriptions, user:read:chat, moderator:read:followers.

2.2. Channel viewers

We receive this data from Twitch because the streamer connected their channel to PhillBot.

Stored in history:

  • channel point reward redemptions: Twitch id, login, name, text, name color, badges;
  • subscription tier and tenure, number of gifted subscriptions over 30 days;
  • "1/2" chat votes during a poll (by id).

Kept only in server memory (never written to disk, gone after a restart):

  • chat messages;
  • follower status.

2.3. Donatello donors

When a streamer connects the Donatello callback, we receive:

  • the name the donor entered;
  • the message;
  • amount and currency;
  • donation id.

2.4. What we do not collect

There is no analytics, advertising or tracking. We hold no payment data — donations go through Donatello.

3. Why, and on what basis

  • Streamers: providing the service (performance of a contract — the Terms of Use).
  • Viewers and donors: the streamer's legitimate interest in running an interactive activity that you chose to take part in (a reward, a donation, a vote).

Data is used only to run these features. We do not sell it or use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Who receives the data

  • Fly.io, Inc. (USA) — hosting. Servers in Frankfurt (Germany), database on an encrypted volume.
  • Twitch — the source of channel and viewer data; also, your browser loads badge and emote images from Twitch's CDN (static-cdn.jtvnw.net), so Twitch sees the visitor's IP address.
  • Donatello — the source of donation data.
  • random.org — we request only random numbers for the wheel from there. No personal data is sent.

Data is not shared with or sold to anyone other than those named above. Because Fly.io is a US company, data may be processed outside Ukraine.

6. How long we keep data

  • History of collections, auctions and donations — 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Access log — 12 months.
  • If a streamer revokes access in Twitch, the token stops working, or an administrator removes them from the list: tokens are deleted immediately, the rest of the channel's data after 30 days unless the streamer signs in again.
  • A streamer can delete all of their channel's data at any time in settings ("Delete channel data").

7. Your rights

  • Access and a copy. A streamer exports a .zip in settings.
  • Correction. A streamer edits data in the interface.
  • Deletion. A streamer deletes it themselves. A viewer or donor sends a request to {{CONTACT}}; we complete it within 30 days, and the name is replaced with "deleted" throughout the history.
  • Objection. You may object to the processing of your data — write to {{CONTACT}}.
  • Complaint. To the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights, or to the EU data protection authority where you live.

So that we can find your data, include your Twitch login (for a donor — the name and approximate date of the donation) and the streamer's channel in your request.

8. Security

  • HTTPS-only connections;
  • Twitch tokens are encrypted, the session cookie is stored only as a hash;
  • access to the service only from the allowlist, database on an encrypted volume.

If a data incident occurs, we will notify Twitch (legal@twitch.tv) and the affected people.

9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13. Streamers must meet Twitch's age requirements.

10. The streamer's duty

A streamer who connects a channel must inform their viewers about data processing, for example by placing a link to {{PUBLIC_URL}}/privacy in their Twitch channel panels.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The version date is shown at the top; we will announce material changes in the service.
